# Jonny's TapTap — How to Play

Welcome to Jonny's TapTap! This guide explains the basics so you can jump in and play.

## Getting Started
1. Open the game.
2. Select a song from the main menu (unlock more in the Shop).
3. Choose a difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Extreme).
4. Tap **Start Game**.

## Controls
- The playfield has 3 or 4 vertical lanes depending on the song.
- Tap the lane when a note reaches the hit line near the bottom.
- Tap accurately to build combo and score.

## Scoring
- **Perfect** hits score highest.
- **Great/Good** hits score less.
- **Miss** breaks your combo and reduces score.

## Note Types
- **Tap**: single tap when it reaches the hit line.
- **Hold**: press and hold until the note ends (if present).
- **Shake**: shake device when the note reaches the hit line (if present).

## Difficulty
- Higher difficulty = faster notes and more notes.
- Use the difficulty menu to switch anytime before starting a song.

## Shop / Unlocks
- Earn Tap Coins by completing songs.
- Spend Tap Coins in the **Shop** to unlock more songs.

## Beatmap Editor (Optional)
- You can create your own beatmaps in-game.
- Import an MP3, pick lanes, play the song, and tap to place notes.
- Your custom beatmaps are saved locally and can be loaded from the main menu.

## Tips
- Focus on rhythm and timing, not just speed.
- Start on Easy and work up as you learn patterns.
- Consistent accuracy is better than random taps.
